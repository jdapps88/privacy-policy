# Privacy Policy – JD Apps

**Last updated:** November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications published by **JD Apps** on Google Play (the "Apps"). The list of covered Apps and any app-specific details can be found at the end of this document.

## 1. Personal Data
Unless otherwise stated in the "Covered Apps" section, the Apps **do not collect, store or share any personal data** that could identify the user.

## 2. Data Storage
Content created by the user within the Apps (such as notes, lists or settings) is stored **only locally on the device**. This data is never sent to the developer's servers and is not accessible to third parties.

## 3. Device Permissions
Some Apps may request Android permissions (for example, camera, storage or notifications). These permissions are used **solely to provide the related feature**, and any data obtained is processed locally on the device. Users can deny or revoke permissions at any time in the Android settings.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Unless otherwise stated in the "Covered Apps" section, the Apps do not use advertising, analytics or any other third-party services that collect information. If an App uses a third-party service, it will be listed below together with a link to that service's privacy policy.

## 5. Data Sharing
The developer does not sell, rent or share user data with third parties.

## 6. Security
Since data remains on the device, its protection also depends on the device's own security measures (for example, screen lock).

## 7. Data Deletion
Users can delete their content within the App at any time. Uninstalling an App removes all associated data from the device. Data can also be cleared via *Settings → Apps → [App] → Storage → Clear data*.

## 8. Children's Privacy
The Apps are not directed at children under the age of 13 and do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 9. Your Rights
Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable laws, you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data. Since the Apps do not collect personal data, the developer holds no information about users. For any questions, please use the contact below.

## 10. Changes to This Policy
This policy may be updated from time to time. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of this page.
